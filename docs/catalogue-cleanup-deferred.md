# Deferred catalogue cleanup — 22 October 2026

**Decision:** Do not remove any catalogue columns or products now. This is a record of findings for a later, coordinated change. The optimiser should continue operating with its current schema.

## 1. Mapping status is still active

`data/products.csv` has a `mapping_status` column with values including `direct`, `direct_variable_weight`, `effective_usable_quantity`, `estimated_usable_weight`, `compatible_candidate` and `substitution_candidate`.

Before removing the column, update these dependencies together:

- `scripts/catalogue.py`: `APPROVED_MAPPINGS`, `MAPPINGS` and `exclusion_reason()` currently decide product eligibility from `mapping_status`. Removing the test without replacing its safety purpose could admit incompatible products.
- `scripts/validate_seed_data.py`: product header requirements, allowed-status checks, the unit compatibility exception for candidate products, and the requirement to document estimated weights refer to the column.
- `scripts/optimise_week.py`: the planning-estimate notice is generated by checking `estimated_usable_weight` and `direct_variable_weight`; retain an estimate warning based on documented pack notes or a deliberate alternative if needed.
- `tests/test_project.py`: tests currently assert that compatibility/substitution candidates are excluded, and a fixture populates `mapping_status`. Update tests to protect ingredient compatibility and unit checks under the replacement design.
- `data/products.csv`: remove the column only after every product has an appropriate `ingredient_id`, matching usable unit and sensible quantities. Audit candidate rows first; some candidates may be genuinely incompatible rather than merely awaiting approval.

**Preferred future approach:** use the product's ingredient ID to identify what it supplies; compare `usable_quantity`/`usable_unit` (if present) or `pack_quantity`/`pack_unit` against the ingredient's preferred model unit. Keep genuinely different ingredients separate. Product alternatives that supply the same ingredient and unit should not require an extra direct/substitution label.

## 2. Other fields

- `product_resolution_status` currently has `resolved_exact` on the reviewed active products. Check all rows and validator/tests before deciding whether it is redundant; unresolved or unverified records must not silently become eligible.
- **Keep** `availability_status` (out-of-stock or unknown products must remain excluded).
- **Keep** `pack_quantity`, `pack_unit`, `usable_quantity`, `usable_unit`: the optimiser already uses usable quantity/unit first, otherwise pack quantity/unit. This supports a weight-labelled pack used as an approximate count.
- **Keep** source links, checked dates and notes for traceability, especially for estimated quantities.
